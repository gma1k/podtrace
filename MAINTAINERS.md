# Maintainers

Review authority for Podtrace is encoded in [CODEOWNERS](CODEOWNERS), which is
the machine-readable source of truth, GitHub enforces it, this file explains
it.

## What maintainers do

- Review and merge pull requests, and are the required approvers under CODEOWNERS.
- Triage incoming issues, apply `area/*` and type labels, and close duplicates.
- Cut releases (see the release flow in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).
- Handle private vulnerability reports under [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- Decide what lands in the public surface governed by [STABILITY.md](STABILITY.md).

## Becoming a maintainer

See [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md#becoming-a-maintainer). In short: sustained,
high-quality contribution and review over time, then an invitation.

## Keeping this file honest

When maintainership changes, update three places together — this file,
[CODEOWNERS](CODEOWNERS), and the repository's collaborator settings. A name
here without a matching CODEOWNERS entry grants no review authority, and a
CODEOWNERS entry without a name here is undocumented power.
